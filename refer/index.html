Refer a patient to Life Care

For health centres, clinics, doctors and humanitarian health services across West Nile. Tell us about your patient before they travel and we will be ready to receive them.

Referral line

+256 7XX XXX XXX

For an emergency transfer, call the 24-hour emergency line instead: +256 7XX XXX XXX

Three steps

  1. Call or send the referral

    Call the referral line, or use the form below, with the patient’s age and sex, your working diagnosis, how urgent the referral is and which service you need.

  2. Send the notes with the patient

    A referral letter with the history, examination findings, observations, test results, any scans, and every medicine given with doses and times.

  3. We receive, treat and reply

    Our team receives the patient, and we send a summary of their care back to you, so follow-up can continue close to home.

Transferring a patient who is very sick

Call the 24-hour emergency line before the patient leaves, so the emergency team, theatre or labour ward can prepare.

  • Stabilise the patient as far as you can before transfer: airway, breathing, circulation, bleeding control and fluids.
  • Send a trained escort where possible, with the referral note and a phone number we can reach you on.
  • For a woman in labour or with bleeding in pregnancy, send the antenatal card and the partograph if one was started.
  • For a child, send the child health card, the weight and any treatment already given.
  • Tell the family what to bring: identification, any insurance card, and money for medicines and food.
